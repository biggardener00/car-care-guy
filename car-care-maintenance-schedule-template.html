How to use this template

Copy the tables below into a notebook, a spreadsheet or a printed sheet. Write the date and mileage (or odometer reading) each time you complete a task. Adjust the intervals to your climate, how often you drive, and your vehicle handbook. This template covers appearance and care checks; it does not replace mechanical servicing, which must follow your vehicle handbook and a qualified mechanic.

Seasonal reminders

  • Hot and sunny: Wash in shade, do not let soap dry, and check protection more often.
  • Wet and muddy: Rinse under arches more often and dry door seals to prevent damp smells.
  • Cold and salty roads: Rinse the underbody and arches as your handbook allows, and clean door seals.
  • Pollen and tree sap season: Remove deposits promptly and wash more often if the car is parked under trees.
